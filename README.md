# Sri Vijaya Clinical Laboratories

Official modern website and diagnostic booking platform for **Sri Vijaya Clinical Laboratories**, delivering clinical accuracy, robotic biochemistry, molecular PCR diagnostics, and gentle doorstep sample collection across West Godavari and Dr. B.R. Ambedkar Konaseema districts since 1997.

---

## 🔬 Key Features & Modules

- **Comprehensive Lab Test Directory (`tests.html`)**: Complete directory of 86+ accredited investigations across 10 clinical disciplines (Hematology, Biochemistry, Thyroid & Hormones, Lipid & Cardiac, Liver LFT, Renal, Serology, Microbiology, Molecular PCR, and Diabetes) with instant category filtering, live keyword search, and parameter detail modal.
- **Preventive Health Checkup Packages (`packages.html`)**: Pathologist-curated full-body screening packages with up to 56% savings and an interactive side-by-side comparison matrix.
- **Doorstep Home Sample Collection (`home-collection.html`)**: Certified phlebotomist booking with sterile BD vacutainers, cold-chain temperature monitoring, and interactive 6-digit PIN code serviceability checker.
- **Dedicated Achievements & Quality Framework (`about.html` / `index.html`)**: Highlighting 25+ years Silver Jubilee leadership, district's first automated laboratory milestone, NABL compliance, ISO 9001:2015 certification, and CMC Vellore & Bio-Rad External Quality Assurance Scheme (EQAS) distinctions.
- **Interactive Facilities Photo Gallery (`gallery.html`)**: Real laboratory infrastructure, Roche and Sysmex automated analyzers, sterile phlebotomy lounges, and medical leadership photos with responsive category filtering and fullscreen Lightbox preview.
- **Modern Contact & Inquiry Portal (`contact.html`)**: Streamlined mobile-friendly inquiry form, emergency 24/7 STAT hotline details, instant WhatsApp prescription upload guidance, and walk-in centre maps.
- **Interactive Multi-Card Reviews Carousel**: Pathologist, physician, and verified patient testimonials with smooth auto-scroll, manual navigation arrows, expanding pill indicators, and touch swipe gestures.

---

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- **HTML5**: Semantic, accessible markup optimized for fast initial paint and search visibility.
- **CSS3**: Modern CSS variables, flexbox and CSS grid layouts, fluid typography (`clamp()`), and smooth cubic-bezier transitions.
- **JavaScript (ES6+)**: Vanilla client-side controller logic, real-time filtering, modal management, LIS cart integration, and touch gesture handling.
- **Icons**: Font Awesome 6 Pro / Free vector iconography.
- **Responsive Design**: Mobile-first architecture tested across 360px, 375px, 412px, 768px, and desktop displays.
