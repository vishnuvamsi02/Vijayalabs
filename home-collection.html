Patient Preparation

Important Guidelines for Accurate Results

Follow these simple preparation steps prior to your home sample collection appointment.

Fasting Requirements (10-12 Hrs)

For Fasting Blood Sugar (FBS), Lipid Profile, or Full Body Health Packages, observe 10 to 12 hours overnight fasting. You may drink plain water, but refrain from tea, coffee, milk, and snacks.

Morning Medication Guidance

Blood pressure medications can typically be taken with a sip of water. For thyroid medicine (Thyronorm/Eltroxin) or diabetes tablets/insulin, take your dose immediately after the blood draw unless advised otherwise by your doctor.

Urine & Routine Specimens

For Complete Urine Examination (CUE), collect the first morning mid-stream urine in a sterile container provided by our phlebotomist or an airtight sterile cup. Keep sample cool until collection.
