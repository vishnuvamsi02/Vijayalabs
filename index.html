Frequently Asked Questions & Patient Guidelines

Everything you need to know about preparing for blood tests, fasting rules, and home sample collection.

Fasting of 10 to 12 hours is required for tests such as Fasting Blood Sugar (FBS), Lipid Profile, and Iron Profile. During fasting, you may drink plain water, but avoid tea, coffee, milk, breakfast, or juices. Tests like Complete Blood Count (CBC), Thyroid Profile (TSH), HbA1c, and Vitamin D3 do NOT require strict fasting.
Home collection is quick and easy: 1) Select your test or package on our website, 2) Choose your preferred date and time slot (morning fasting slots start from 6:00 AM), 3) Our certified, barcoded phlebotomist arrives at your doorstep with single-use sterile vacuum tubes and a cold-chain transport carrier, 4) You receive your digital report on WhatsApp and SMS within 4 to 6 hours.
